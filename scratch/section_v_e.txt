E. A Methodological Finding: Metric Definition Errors  
During iterative development, an internal evaluation utility was 
found to report a quantity labeled "mAP@50" that varied 
continuously with the confidence threshold supplied to it (for 
example, 94.28% at threshold 0.10 rising monotonically to 96.87% 
at threshold 0.35 on a validation split). Standard mAP@50, as 
defined by the precision -recall integral in Eq. (1) -(2) below and as 
computed by the Ultralytics validation API used for every number 
in Tables I -IV threshold -independent is by construction because 
threshold -independent integrates precision over the recall range for 
each class, before averaging:  
AP = ∫₀¹ P(R) dR, mAP  = (1/M) Σ AP(i) (1) -(2) 
A metric that changes materially with a manually supplied 
confidence threshold is, by this definition, not mAP; it is more likely 
a precision/recall/F1 figure computed at a single fixed operating 
point and mislabeled. We flag this not to single out any one  script, 
but because we believe it is a common and under -reported failure 
mode in applied PCB -inspection literature and internal tooling alike: 
any accuracy claim that cites a confidence threshold alongside 
"mAP " merits independent re -verification with the standard, 
threshold -integrated computation before being trusted or published. 
All figures in Tables I -IV were produced only after this re -
verification step.
